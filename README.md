# Hack The Box & CTF Writeups 🚩

Welcome to my repository of Hack The Box (HTB) and Capture The Flag (CTF) documentation. This space serves as a portfolio of my practical experience in offensive security, vulnerability analysis, and digital forensics. 

As a Cybersecurity Junior at the University of South Florida, I use these simulated environments to bridge the gap between academic theory and hands-on penetration testing.
